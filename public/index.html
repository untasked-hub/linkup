How it works

  1. Create a room and get a link instantly.
  2. Send it over any chat app — the recipient just taps it.
  3. Connect and the call starts automatically in the browser.

Privacy, in plain words

  • Video and audio go directly between devices. The people in the call can see your IP address, as with any peer-to-peer call. LinkUp does not relay your media.
  • LinkUp does not record calls. Nothing is saved from the video or audio.
  • The server keeps only what it needs. Room details, a hashed passcode if you set one, and the names people entered when they joined. Rooms are deleted after 24 hours.
  • Rooms stay private by default. A room appears in the public list only if its host chooses to list it, and locked rooms never appear.
